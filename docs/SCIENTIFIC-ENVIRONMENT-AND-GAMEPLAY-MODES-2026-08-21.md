# qFoldIT Scientific Environment & Gameplay Modes

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Cross-engine capability contract alignment

UNIGINE is the preferred industrial/engineering surface for rich environmental simulation and scientific visualization.

## Scientific environment

The adapter aligns to `qfoldit.scientific-environment/1.0` for:

- particles and particulate transport;
- fluids and liquid-process visualization;
- volumetric media;
- scalar/vector fields;
- thermal, pressure and energy fields;
- diffusion, dissolution and reaction effects;
- crystal, mineral, bio and reactive materials;
- transparent and physically based materials.

## Gameplay modes

In addition to research/arena/co-op/sandbox/validation, UNIGINE missions should support:

- **Build Mode** for constrained engineering construction;
- **Racing Mode** for route, timing, geometry and process optimization.

## Quantum optimization

QOS policy can choose classical, opportunistic quantum, checkpoint, plateau-breaker or hybrid ensemble execution. Every invocation is recorded as a mission state transition.

## Runtime boundary

UNIGINE renders and simulates the mission world; scientific authority remains with external validators and customer-authorized domain services.
